# Welcome and Expectations

## Welcome!

Welcome to the **Azure Data Engineering Bootcamp**, a free, live, online program that takes you from cloud basics to a working end-to-end data project. Here is what to expect and what we expect from you.

## What you will learn

| Session | Topic | You will be able to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | Cloud Fundamentals | Explain cloud computing; create your free Azure account |
| **2** | Storage Account and ADF | Create storage accounts and a data factory; explain OLTP vs OLAP |
| **3** | ADF Implementation | Build pipelines that copy data from Azure SQL Database to a data lake |
| **4** | ADF Pipelines | Make pipelines dynamic; use Parquet; copy many tables at once |
| **5** | Databricks | Read data, create tables and ask questions with Genie |
| **6** | Resume and Interview | Write a stronger resume and practise interview questions |
| **7** | What Next? | Plan your certification and career next steps |

We use **Azure Data Factory**, **Azure Data Lake Storage Gen2** and **Databricks**, and we finish with guidance towards the Microsoft **DP-750** certification.

**In every session there will be many quizzes and assignments** to check your understanding and to practise on your own.

## How the sessions run

- **Where:** Google Meet, on a laptop or desktop with Google Chrome for the best experience.
- **Session 1:** Sunday, September 20, 2026, 7:00 PM CAT (Rwanda). Please join 5 to 10 minutes early.
- **Format:** short explanations, live demonstrations and hands-on practice, with time for questions.
- **Quizzes and assignments:** every session includes quizzes to check what you learned and assignments to practise between sessions.

## What we expect from you

- **Show up and be on time.** Live attendance is where you can ask questions and follow along.
- **Practise between sessions.** The hands-on work is where the learning happens.
- **Do the quizzes and assignments.** They are how you find your gaps and build real skill. Submit assignments on time, as your instructor explains.
- **Ask questions.** If you are confused, someone else is too. Use the chat or speak up.
- **Be honest about your level.** There are no silly questions here.
- **Respect others.** Be kind, listen, and keep the chat and the group focused on learning.
- **Protect your privacy.** Never share passwords, storage keys, connection strings or card details in the chat or in the group.
- **Watch your cloud costs.** Stop or delete what you no longer need. We will show you how.
- **Keep notes.** Write down the steps and settings you use, so you can repeat them alone.

## What you can expect from us

- Clear explanations, in plain language, from the basics.
- Live, step-by-step demonstrations you can follow on your own computer.
- Time for your questions in every session, and a longer Q&A near the end.
- Quizzes and assignments in every session, with answers explained.
- Honest answers, including “I need to check that”, and no exaggerated promises about jobs or exam results.

## Before Session 1

- Check the time-zone list above and set a reminder for your local time.
- Use a laptop or desktop with Google Chrome.
- Have your phone nearby for a verification code.
- Use an email address you will keep using for the bootcamp. A personal email is simplest.
- Have a notebook or note-taking app ready.

## How to ask for help

- During a session: type your question in the chat, or raise it when the host pauses. This includes quiz questions.
- Between sessions, including for assignments: post in the group, with a screenshot of the error but no passwords or keys.

**Learn · Build · Grow.** We look forward to seeing you!
